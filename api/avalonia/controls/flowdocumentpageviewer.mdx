A read-only viewer that displays a [Avalonia.Controls.Documents.FlowDocument](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.FlowDocument) as discrete
pages (Word's print layout), scrolled as a continuous vertical stack of sheets.
Inherits the selection and copy behavior of [Avalonia.Controls.FlowDocumentScrollViewer](xref:Avalonia.Controls.FlowDocumentScrollViewer);
the template hosts a [Avalonia.Controls.Documents.Primitives.PagedTextView](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.Primitives.PagedTextView) instead of the continuous view.

## 备注 {#remarks}

[Avalonia.Controls.FlowDocumentPageViewer.PageCount](xref:Avalonia.Controls.FlowDocumentPageViewer.PageCount) is progressive: pagination runs at idle priority and the
count grows until [Avalonia.Controls.FlowDocumentPageViewer.IsPaginationComplete](xref:Avalonia.Controls.FlowDocumentPageViewer.IsPaginationComplete). Bind a pager
control (e.g. `PipsPager`) to [Avalonia.Controls.FlowDocumentPageViewer.PageCount](xref:Avalonia.Controls.FlowDocumentPageViewer.PageCount) and
[Avalonia.Controls.FlowDocumentPageViewer.CurrentPageNumber](xref:Avalonia.Controls.FlowDocumentPageViewer.CurrentPageNumber) for page navigation UI.
